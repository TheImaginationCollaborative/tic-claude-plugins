# Laying out an event site

## Ask, do not assume
Get these from the user or the Event Brief before proposing a layout:
- Expected crowd, peak and total, and the kind of event.
- The site boundary, or a survey or aerial to work over.
- Where the public, vehicles and emergency services come in.
- Stages, screens and other focal points, and which way they face.
- Licence or authority conditions the site must meet.
- Which guidance applies where the event is.

## Figures
- Take toilets, water, exits, bins, first aid and similar figures from the `event_calculator` tool, or from guidance the user names. Never invent a ratio, width or capacity.
- Say every figure is planning guidance only, and must be checked with a relevant professional for the user's own event.

## Order of work
1. Boundary and grid.
2. Access and egress: perimeter fence, gates, emergency routes kept clear.
3. Focal points.
4. Crowd areas and queues in front of bars, food and entries.
5. Services: amenities, food trucks, generators, infrastructure, cable ramps where cables cross a route.
6. Tents and structures.
7. Refresh Suite Data, then the reports the user wants.
8. Sheets: grid sheets or viewports, then Update Sheets.

## Check as you go
- Look after each stage and say what you see.
- Point out anything that may block an emergency route or a gate, as something for the user to check. It is never a safety approval.
- Claude proposes; the user decides.
